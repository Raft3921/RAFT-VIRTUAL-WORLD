# Four shared combat maps — maps121

The room can choose four maps: the existing sand town (`sand`), a staggered snow town (`snow`), a desert highland town (`sand-large`), and a snow citadel (`snow-large`). Each large map has exactly five times the original footprint area: both horizontal dimensions grow by sqrt(5), from 124 × 96 m to approximately 277.3 × 214.7 m. The west staging position stays fixed so world-only and combat-only entrances remain consistent.

The three new maps have different street patterns, building positions, tower distributions and terrace heights. New normal snow has 12 building sites; the large desert has 48 and the large snow has 42. Tower roofs are reached through alternating exterior stair flights and connected landings. Large towers and raised districts reach approximately 30–44 m; selected rooftops have stepped skywalks. Raised districts have walkable lower passages with multiple mouths, sheltered beneath the upper district. These are underground-style routes under elevated terrain, at world floor height, rather than excavation into negative coordinates. Normal sand retains its existing layout. Snow uses pale masonry, roof snow, drifts, trees and a bounded single-draw snowfall effect, suppressed under roofs and during the final overview.

Map selection is available from the reusable world-menu board and from gun entry/exit boards. It is a saved, room-wide setting; guests cannot change it. Participants must exit before a switch. Switching saves settings first, clears arena footprints and destructible traces/projectiles, rebuilds authoritative geometry, moves affected players to staging, and broadcasts a snapshot that reloads connected clients. Saved coordinates include the map ID and cannot restore an old combat position into another map's walls. Other world positions remain usable.

Shared immutable map definitions feed render geometry, movement bounds, server movement, bullet collision, props, spawn selection, exit-board validation, environment grass exclusion and final replay overview. Worker/LAN bootstrap endpoints expose the selected map before rendering. Map geometry is selected per world/room, without a process-global active map. New geometry uses spatial batches/instancing, continuous collision ramps and cached spawn/prop tables.

No tests, syntax checks, browser/site checks, visual checks or multiplayer verification were performed, following the user's earlier instruction. Implementation and publication do not establish actual navigation, appearance or performance acceptance.
